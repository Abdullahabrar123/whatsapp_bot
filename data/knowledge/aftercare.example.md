# Aftercare advice

After a scale and polish your gums may feel tender for up to 48 hours. Rinse
with warm salty water twice a day and avoid very hot or very cold drinks for
the first day.

# Whitening aftercare

Avoid tea, coffee, red wine and tobacco for 48 hours after whitening, as teeth
absorb staining most readily during this period. Mild sensitivity is normal and
usually settles within three days.

# Missed appointment

If you miss an appointment without notice we may charge 50% of the treatment
fee. Contact reception as soon as possible to rearrange.
